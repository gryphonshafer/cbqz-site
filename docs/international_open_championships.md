# International Open Championships (IOC)

<!-- docs:hide_photos -->

**Christian Bible Quizzing (CBQ)** invites you to participate in the **International Open Championships (IOC) 2027**. IOC is an exciting season-end championship meet where quizzers from a variety of Quizzing traditions quiz together, enveloped in an environment of fellowship, fun, and festivities.

- **Dates**: Friday-Sunday, May 21-23, 2027
    - *Arrive*: Friday afternoon
    - *Quizzing*: Friday evening after dinner; Saturday morning; Saturday afternoon
    - *Optional*: Saturday night fellowship time, concluding with Sunday morning devotions and breakfast
- **Location**: [Black Lake Bible Camp](https://blbcolympia.com)
    - *Address*: [6521 Fairview Rd SW, Olympia WA 98512](https://google.com/maps/dir//6521%20Fairview%20Rd%20SW,%20Olympia%20WA%2098512)

**This meet is an open invitational.** All who are interested in participating are invited and encouraged to submit their interest. The goal of the meet is to Bible Quiz across many programs and styles, building bridges and advancing interest in Bible Quizzing.

## Notify Us of Your Interest ASAP

**Don't miss out** on this opportunity to fellowship with Quizzing people you might otherwise never meet and to experience what we're working hard to make the most fun quiz meet in CBQ history.

There’s limited space, so please email us at [ioc@cbqz.org](mailto:ioc@cbqz.org) as soon as possible to let us know of your interest in participating, either as a team or individual. This doesn’t constitute a commitment, but rather a non-binding reservation. **We need to hear from you prior to <span style="color: #c00">Monday, November 30, 2026</span>** to ensure you don’t miss out.

## Common Questions

> What’s the age range or age limits for participating in IOC?

There's no official age range or limit, though IOC leadership feels the meet is best suited for quizzers in middle school and high school. However, it's entirely up to coaches and programs to evaluate the maturity level and readiness of their quizzers.

> Will there be an adult division?

At this time, we’re not planning an adult division. However, if there are enough adults interested in participating in an adult division, we should have plenty of quiz room options at the camp to run a parallel adult division. If you’re interested in this, please email us at [ioc@cbqz.org](mailto:ioc@cbqz.org) as soon as possible.

> What will be the material for the IOC 2027 meet?

The upper youth division will be the Gospel of John in full. We may deploy material label weights for other divisions depending on the nature and depth of memorization across the set of quizzers who ultimately register.

### The Pines

The Pines is a new cabin facility at BLBC, located on the outskirts of Harmony Springs. It consists of 4 individual cabins, each with 2 sleeping quarters, a living room, and space for small to mid-sized groups. Each sleeping quarter has 4 bunk beds and a queen bed, totaling 9 beds per room and 18 beds per cabin. The cabins feature 2 sets of accessible restrooms and a small common room for group gatherings.
